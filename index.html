Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

FinishLine Snap Timer ("the App") is a mobile application that helps users time runners at a finish line and capture photographs of runners with their finish times.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Information We Access

Camera

The App requests access to the device camera so that users can take photographs of runners at the finish line.

Photos taken using the App remain on the user's device. The App does not upload, transmit, or share photographs with the developer or with third parties.

Photos and Media

The App may save photographs taken by the user to the device's local photo storage or designated photo folder.

The developer does not have access to these photographs.

Users can manage or delete photographs using the device's normal photo and file-management features.

Information We Collect

FinishLine Snap Timer does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the App.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information, photographs, names, email addresses, phone numbers, location information, or other personal information.

The App does not transmit photographs or other user-generated content to the developer or to a remote server.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user information.

Because photographs and other content created with the App remain on the user's device, they are not transmitted to the developer or third parties by the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not maintain a remote database containing user information or photographs.

Photographs created using the App remain on the user's device until the user deletes them.

Users can delete photographs through their device's photo or file-management applications.

Uninstalling the App may remove App-specific data stored on the device, subject to the device's operating system behavior.

Children's Privacy

FinishLine Snap Timer is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Data Security

Because the App does not transmit photographs or personal information to the developer or to remote servers, the App does not maintain such information remotely.

Users are responsible for securing the photographs and other information stored on their own devices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
